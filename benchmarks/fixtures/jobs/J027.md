The role

Retail Loan Officers (RLOs) are key drivers of SoFi Lending Corp.’s mortgage sales organization. RLOs are responsible for delivering an exceptional, end-to-end customer experience by guiding members through the mortgage origination process and providing a modern, transparent alternative to the traditional mortgage experience.

What you’ll do:

- Evaluate initial loan applications and supporting documentation to assess creditworthiness and repayment ability

- Perform preliminary analysis of applicants’ credit, income, and assets, communicating initial eligibility and next steps clearly to the customer

- Communicate key origination timelines, product options, pricing, and underwriting requirements throughout the loan lifecycle

- Proactively engage with applicants through outbound communication to obtain required documentation and ensure timely progression from application to funding

- Clearly communicate loan status updates, conditions, approvals, and/or declinations to ensure customers are informed at every stage

- Coordinate with realtors, builders, mobile notaries, and other third-party partners to support a smooth transaction

- Manage the loan process to ensure timely and accurate delivery of disclosures, re-disclosures due to changed circumstances, and closing documentation

- Continuously enhance sales effectiveness and customer experience through ongoing training and development

- Maximize monthly funded loan volume while maintaining quality and compliance standards

- Manage and convert a pipeline of centrally provided leads while delivering a high-touch, consultative retail experience
