# Designer, Women's Cut and Sew Knits

Clean luxury. Better for you. At Everlane, we believe luxury should be as effortless as it is conscious. We partner with ethical factories, work with premium, sustainably sourced materials, and strive to leave the industry cleaner than we found it. There's still much to do, and we're excited to grow a team of motivated humans ready for the challenge. The journey toward better continues.

At Everlane we are product leaders.  We are building a first-class design team, and we are currently looking for a Designer that will focus on designing and developing great products for Women’s Everlane Customers. This includes shirting, dresses, casual and tailored bottoms, as well as support on both technical and tailored outerwear.

As the Designer - Women’s Cut and Sew knits, you must be passionate, enthusiastic, and rigorous, with an obsessive eye for detail. You will bring your designs to life by reviewing samples, attending fittings, and collaborating with product development, merchandising, and production teams. Our designs are seen by hundreds of thousands of people and are consistently featured in popular fashion publications. Our brand ethos and design aesthetic is one of purity and simplicity; less is more. Our vision is to create perfect and timeless wardrobe pieces for every woman. We rely on a thorough editing process to get these essential pieces just right, from fabric to fit, to drape and construction.

*Please Note: A portfolio is required to be considered for this position. Please ensure that you provide a link to your portfolio on your resume.*

- Support in the creation of female knit products

- Initiate designs beginning with thorough research and inspirational images

- Work closely with other members of the design team to develop cohesive seasonal product

- 3+ years of experience in a similar role

- Technical knowledge of knits construction

- Bachelor's Degree in Fashion or closely related design field

- Great taste and a strong understanding of casual chic

- The desire to work on a collaborative and diverse team that is dedicated to doing good for the planet and the consumer

- An entrepreneurial mindset and a solution-oriented approach to challenges
