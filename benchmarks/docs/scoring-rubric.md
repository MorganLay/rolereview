# Scoring rubric

Total 100. Scores are entered by a person after reading both resumes. A deterministic check may record that a forbidden claim appears. It does not assign the 100-point score.

| Category | Max | What a high score requires |
|---|---:|---|
| Factual accuracy | 30 | Claims trace to the stored resume. Invented credentials, licences, metrics, titles, or tools score at the bottom. A critical invention such as a licence the person does not hold is flagged separately and the case is not a success. |
| Relevance | 25 | Important posting requirements that the resume actually supports are visible. Keyword stuffing and irrelevant roles used as if they were the job score low. |
| Writing | 20 | Sentences are specific and readable. Vague lines that the source can support may be clarified. New facts may not be added to make a line sound stronger. |
| Prioritization | 15 | The strongest relevant evidence leads. A buried accomplishment or a lead role that does not serve the posting scores low. |
| Formatting | 10 | One readable order, consistent dates where dates exist, and extractable text. This is not an ATS hiring prediction. |

Each saved score needs a rationale, evidence identifiers, and a confidence of high, medium, or low. An empty rationale is not a score. If the reviewer cannot point at the resume and the posting, the judgment stays unreviewed.

The direct baseline and RoleRefine are scored on the same anchors. Equal-evidence runs and end-to-end runs are reported separately.
