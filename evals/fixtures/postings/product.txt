Keel & Co — Associate product analyst

Toronto, Ontario. Hybrid, two days a week with the product team. Full-time. $75,000–$88,000 a year.

Keel & Co makes software that independent grocers use for specials and pickup orders. This is not a coding role. You will work with a product manager and two engineers.

What you will do
- Talk to store managers, write down what they asked for, and turn that into a short requirement.
- Keep the backlog in order with the product manager, and say which item should wait.
- Pull simple counts with SQL or Excel so a decision is based on orders, not a guess.
- Walk a store manager through a change before it ships, and note what confused them.
- Write the release note in plain language.

Required
- Experience explaining a process to customers or to colleagues who do not build the product. Retail, operations, or analytics backgrounds are welcome.
- Excel, including a pivot table.
- A writing sample, which can be a school project, a help article, or a requirements note.
- Comfortable learning a basic SQL query with help from an analyst.

Assets
- A small product, catalogue, or school project you can describe.
- Experience reconciling orders, cash, or another operational list.
- Power BI or another dashboard you have used as a reader.

Apply with a resume. Tell us about one time you changed a process after listening to a customer. The posting closes on 8 January 2027.
