Lakeshore Mutual — Data analyst

Toronto, Ontario. Hybrid, two days a week in the office. Full-time. $70,000–$85,000 a year.

Lakeshore Mutual is a fictional regional insurer. The analytics team turns policy and claims data into reports for branch managers.

What you will do
- Write SQL against a Postgres warehouse to answer questions from operations.
- Build and maintain dashboards in Power BI or Tableau.
- Check data quality before a report is published, and document the definition of each metric.
- Walk branch managers through a weekly pack and teach them how to use the filters.
- Keep an Excel checklist for the files that still arrive by email.

Required
- Two years of experience in reporting, analytics, or a similar data role.
- Strong SQL.
- Excel, including lookups and pivot tables.
- Experience presenting numbers to people who do not write queries.
- Clear written English.

Assets
- Power BI or Tableau.
- Python for file checks.
- Experience in insurance, banking, or another regulated setting.

Apply with a resume. The posting closes on 15 December 2026.
