Brightpath Hosting — Support engineer

Remote within Canada. Full-time. $58,000–$68,000 a year.

Brightpath Hosting provides email and small-site hosting for clubs and local businesses. The support desk is a team of five.

What you will do
- Answer customer email and the ticket queue about mail delivery, DNS, and password resets.
- Follow a runbook for the common Linux checks, and escalate when the runbook stops.
- Write or update a help article when the same question arrives three times.
- Take part in a shared on-call rotation one week a month, with a senior engineer as backup.
- Keep notes in the ticket so the next person can see what was already tried.

Required
- One year of customer support, help desk, or another role where you explained technical steps in writing.
- Comfort reading a log file and a knowledge-base article.
- Clear written English.
- Reliable home internet for remote work.

Assets
- Linux or another command line.
- HTML, enough to spot a broken link in a customer's page.
- Experience with DNS or email delivery.
- A second language used with customers.

No degree is required. Apply with a resume. The posting closes on 12 December 2026.
