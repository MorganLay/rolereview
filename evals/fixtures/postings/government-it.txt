City of Northwater — Information technology developer

Competition NW-IT-2026-014. On site at Northwater City Hall. Full-time, permanent. Salary $78,400–$92,100 a year.

The City of Northwater is fictional. The information technology branch maintains internal systems for permits, recreation bookings, and staff directories.

What you will do
- Develop and maintain features in Java or C# services and their web front ends.
- Write SQL for reporting views used by clerks.
- Design small REST endpoints and document them for the team.
- Fix defects logged by city staff and explain the change in plain language.
- Follow the city's change-control checklist before a release.

Required
- A degree or diploma in computer science or a related field, or an equivalent combination of study and work.
- Two years of software development experience.
- SQL and a relational database.
- Experience with Git.
- Ability to write short technical notes for non-developers.

Assets
- REST APIs.
- Automated tests.
- English and French.
- Experience in a government or other public-sector setting. A criminal-record check is required after a conditional offer. It is not part of the resume screen.
