Kindling Studio — Frontend developer

Montreal, Quebec. Hybrid, three days in the studio. Full-time. $95,000–$115,000 a year.

Kindling Studio designs and builds marketing and account sites for cultural organizations. This is a mid-level role on a team of six.

What you will do
- Build interfaces in React and TypeScript.
- Extend a shared component library and document components for other developers.
- Write CSS that matches designs from Figma, including keyboard and screen-reader behaviour.
- Add automated checks with Jest or Playwright for sign-in and other critical flows.
- Review pull requests and pair with a designer when a layout is unclear.

Required
- About three years of professional web development.
- TypeScript, React, HTML, and CSS.
- Git, and a habit of small pull requests.
- One shipped example of an accessibility fix, described in your resume.

Assets
- Storybook or another component workshop.
- Experience with design systems used by more than one team.
- French is useful and not required.

Apply with a resume and a link to two pages you built. No cover letter is required.
