## App shell

- **Sidebar** (top bar + menu on mobile): Dashboard · Library · Jobs board · Contacts · Insights · Settings. Bottom: Usage pill ("2 / 3 tailored resumes"; plus "N extra" when tailored-resume bonus credits remain) → Settings → Plan (`/settings?tab=plan`). On a wide screen the sidebar is one viewport tall and stays in view while the page scrolls; the pill stays at the bottom of that sidebar. On a phone it is at the bottom of the menu. [1.1] XP bar and level.
- **Header:** reminders bell with the count of open reminders that are due today or overdue (popover: Done, Snooze 1 day, Dismiss, and Open job); account menu. The trigger is an icon. The email is inside the menu, with Sign out (and Invite friends [1.1]). On a phone the menu also includes Feedback. **New job** sits in this header, before the reminders bell, and opens a blank form at `/jobs/new`. It is not shown on `/jobs/new`. The Jobs board toolbar does not repeat it. The empty board still offers New job.
- **Global:** toaster; Upgrade modal (opened by any `QUOTA_EXCEEDED` or Pro-only action); Feedback opens a small form → `Feedback`. On a phone it is in the account menu. On a wider screen it is in the sidebar, above the usage pill, so it does not cover the page. A payment-failed banner when Stripe reports the subscription as past due or unpaid, with a link to Plan & billing to update the payment method; [1.1] milestone toast. Dialogs stay within the visible height and scroll, including when the on-screen keyboard is open.

## Flow A — first-time user (activation)

1. Homepage CTA → `/sign-in?next=/library/import` → magic link (Supabase admin generates it, Resend sends it, five per email per hour) or Google → `/auth/callback` (session only) → first app load `requireUser()` creates the User (stores `signupSource`, `utm*`, `signupReferrer` from the first-touch cookie; `timezone` from the browser via `setTimezone`).
2. Lands on `/library/import` with: "Start your library from a resume. Nothing is saved until you save it to your library."
3. After saving the import → `/dashboard` shows the **Getting started** checklist (4 steps, computed from data):
   1. Build your library (≥ 1 entry)
   2. Accept a job (≥ 1 job)
   3. Tailor your resume (≥ 1 READY tailored resume)
   4. Export it (any tailored resume with `exportedAt`)
   Completing all four sets `onboardingCompletedAt`, shows a brief success message, and [1.1] unlocks the "First tailored resume" milestone.

## Flow B — daily use

Dashboard (what to do next, where you left off, and how the search is going) → complete a reminder or log a follow-up → paste a new posting → tailor a resume → tweak → export → drag the card to Applied (auto follow-up reminder) → later log an interview (auto prep and thank-you reminders) → record the offer → Accepted 🎉.

## Screen specs

### `/dashboard` — Dashboard
The page title is **Dashboard**, with “Your job search, at a glance.” The header **New job** stays. Until the four setup counts are done, this page is **Getting started**: **Set up your job search.** The card uses the same surface as the rest of the dashboard. The current step is marked and says what it is for. **Build your library** opens Library. **Add a job** opens a blank posting. **Tailor your resume** opens that saved job’s documents when a saved job is waiting on a resume, and the jobs board otherwise. **Export it** opens the resume when one is ready to send, and the jobs board otherwise. A resume that is still being written sits beside the checklist. The sections below stay off this page. When all four counts are true and `onboardingCompletedAt` is empty, that visit sets it, shows “You’re set up. This page now shows your search at a glance.”, and those sections replace the checklist. Sections that have nothing to say are left out.

After setup, the page is these sections:

1. **Next step.** With no entries and no skills, the card is **Start with your experience.** **Import resume** opens `/library/import`, **New job** opens a blank posting, and **Add manually** opens `/library`. Once the library has an entry or a skill, the card is **Found an interesting role?** with **New job**. It does not contain the posting form.
2. **Continue where you left off.** Shown only when there is unfinished work. A resume still being written (PENDING, and not yet the stale tailor window) comes first and opens Documents. Otherwise only a Saved job qualifies, in this order: newest ready resume not exported (Review resume), analysis ready and no ready resume (Tailor resume), newest ready resume already exported (Preview & export), analysis not ready (Open application, the job page). The same rank uses the latest update, then the lower id. An Applied, Screening, Interviewing, or Offer job is not asked to review a resume because that resume is unsubmitted. Archived and closed jobs stay out. If nothing qualifies, the section is omitted.
3. **Your library.** Omitted when the library card above is the empty one. Otherwise one line for Strong or Excellent bullets when any exist, then one next step: wording (the same capped list as the library page, at most five), else evidence, else completeness under 100%. Completeness is not writing quality. **Improve library** or **Open library** goes to `/library`. No new analysis call.
4. **Applications.** Shown when at least one job is not archived. Four figures: Active (not Accepted, Rejected, Withdrawn, or No response), Sent this week (`appliedAt`), Interviews (status Interviewing), Tailored this week (READY resumes created this week). **View jobs board** opens `/jobs`. This week is Monday 00:00 through the next Monday in the user’s timezone. The share of applications sent with a submitted resume is still computed and is not shown.
5. **Needs your attention.** Shown once there is a non-archived job. Empty is one sentence, not a card. Rows are the dated action list, earliest first, with the same tie order (deadline, offer respond-by, interview, follow-up). An unfinished resume is not a row. An Applied or Screening job with no open follow-up, thank-you, or custom reminder is a row only after 30 days with no response. Each row names the action, the company and role, and why, and opens that specific tab or resume. Closed jobs stay out. No row links to the jobs board, the dashboard, or a job with no tab and no resume.
6. **Recent applications.** Up to five jobs that are not archived, newest `updatedAt` first, one row each. The role, company, status, and a date: a deadline that is past or within three days, otherwise the applied date, otherwise a later deadline, otherwise the updated date. The row opens that job.

Snooze, Done, and Dismiss still live on the reminder itself (header bell and the job’s Reminders tab). Snooze sets the due time to exactly 24 hours from now. A dismissed reminder leaves the lists. Urgent, for anything still counting it, is unchanged: a deadline or respond-by on today or earlier, an interview within 48 hours, or a follow-up due before the next local midnight. The page does not show that count.

### `/library`
- **Profile and quality summary first, then Experience.** Under the page title, a compact profile strip and a horizontal quality summary sit stacked (full width). Experience follows higher in the viewport as the dominant list. Projects, education, certifications, volunteer, other, and skills come after. The page uses the full main column.
- **Profile card** (header data) with an edit dialog: name, headline, email, phone, location, links (≤ 5), default summary. On the card, the name leads on one line with headline and location beside it when space allows; the summary is one line. Headline and summary variants stay behind a compact alternate-wording control until opened; suggesting and approving variants is unchanged. A missing profile field named in the quality details opens that field.
- **Library quality summary:** four compact metrics in a row from `lg` (two columns from `sm`, stacked below that): **Completeness** (five checks, 20% each — profile complete: name, headline, location, and summary — phone and links are optional; ≥ 1 Experience entry; ≥ 5 skills; ≥ 3 bullets on every recent role; ≥ 50% of all bullets Good or better, score ≥ 4), **Writing quality** (“N of M bullets Strong+”, score 5 or 6), **Wording** (lines that need clearer wording), and **Evidence** (Good-or-better accomplishment lines that still lack a concrete number or named tool). Completeness is not writing quality: 100% complete can still show wording or evidence opportunities. Inventing a metric does not raise completeness. Details (missing profile fields, failed checks, and the completeness-vs-quality note) open on demand. A recent role is every current Experience entry; if none is current, it is the single Experience entry with the latest start (`YYYY` ranks before any month in that year, and a later year beats an earlier month). Ties use the lower `sortOrder`, then id. Zero bullets fails the Good+ check. Education, certification, and coursework are records: they are not judged as professional accomplishments, and a line that already has a verb is not told to add one. Missing information, writing clarity, and evidence stay separate. The strength function still returns a score of 0–6.
- **Sections** in fixed order (Experience, Projects, Education, Certifications, Volunteer, Other). A section with no entries says “None yet.” and offers add. Entry card: title as the strongest line, then organization, location, and dates, optional URL, a compact bullet count, Edit, expand/collapse, and a menu for move up/down and delete. Move up/down stays within the section. Delete still asks for confirmation, then shows Undo for 8 seconds. The entry leaves the list. Undo puts it back and does not delete it. When the toast ends, or is dismissed without Undo, the entry is deleted. Leaving the page while Undo is open deletes it. If the delete fails, the error shows and the entry comes back. Entry dialog: title, organization, location, URL, start and end as an optional month plus a year (`YYYY-MM` or `YYYY`), and a current checkbox (stores the end as present).
- **Bullets:** each row is the text (edit on click), a quieter strength meter with the overflow menu (move up/down, delete), then any improvement action under the text. From `sm`, the meter and menu sit to the right of the text. Inline edit (Enter or blur saves, Esc cancels, 400-character counter). Text is trimmed and required, at most 400 characters. Add, delete without a confirmation dialog, and move up/down. Delete shows Undo for 8 seconds. The bullet leaves the list. Undo puts it back and does not delete it. When the toast ends, or is dismissed without Undo, the bullet is deleted. Leaving the page while Undo is open deletes it. If the delete fails, the error shows and the bullet comes back. The meter still uses five segments with a text label (Weak · Fair · Good · Strong · Excellent) and reasons on hover or keyboard focus. Strong and Excellent use a quieter pine tint so the grade stays secondary to the text.
- **Bullet improvement** sits on the bullet only when the strength checks show a material opportunity. The label matches the opportunity (Clarify wording, Strengthen evidence, Explore more detail), with a small icon and pine weight so it reads as an action. Alternate wording links stay quieter slate metadata. Excellent lines with no issue get no prompt. Opening one improvement panel closes any other on that experience. The panel shows the existing diagnosis, original and suggested wording side by side when space allows, why, and an optional question. Accept replaces that library bullet. Undo lasts 8 seconds and restores the previous text only while the bullet still has the accepted text. Existing tailored resumes keep their snapshots. If a metric would help, the suggestion asks a question and adds no new digit. A failed call does not change the bullet and does not count against the allowance. A call the model completes still counts when the guard drops the suggestion. The allowance is the existing bullet-coach meter: Free 10 and Pro 300 per month. The three-suggestion coach with placeholders is still later.
- **Find missing details** sits in the experience footer beside Add bullet. It asks discovery questions from the title, responsibilities, bullets, projects, and skills. Those questions are hypotheses. Nothing is saved until the person confirms it. “Doesn’t apply” skips a question. A yes, and any detail they type, become confirmed evidence for the draft. After a confirmation, at most two follow-up questions (earlier answers are kept), then one suggested bullet the person can edit and add. The thin original bullet stays.
- **Overlap hint.** Within one experience, a precise local check can flag likely overlapping bullets (shared content words, high Jaccard). The hint is discreet, on-demand to open, and never deletes or merges. It does not call the model on page load.
- **Approved wording.** A bullet, headline, or summary can keep more than one accurate wording. The canonical text stays on the bullet, the headline, or the default summary. A variant records whether it is the earlier wording, a proposal, an approved line, a job-specific line, or the person's own edit, plus why and the source ids. One headline and one reusable summary are the default. Suggesting headlines or a summary again replaces the unapproved suggestions. Approving one closes the list. A headline is a short focus under the name, at most eight words. It is not a bullet and not a list of tools. It does not repeat one past job title. A summary written for one posting is stored for that job and does not replace the reusable summary. This is not a target profile.
- **Tailoring** writes a finished resume for this posting. The summary and the selected bullets already use the model's wording. It may keep a library bullet, use an approved variant, or write a job-specific line. A specific sentence is stored when the line changed, with what changed and the requirement ids that motivated it. A vague note is not stored. A variant is not given its own match percentage. Wording changes do not raise the requirement score. A new confirmed library bullet can change later coverage. A tailored bullet cites every library bullet it uses, including when several facts from the same role are written as one line. Two imported records of that role share one heading, and a line may cite either record. Comparing a combined line shows each source. Restoring a combined line puts those sources back as separate bullets, in library order, with each source's own wording. A bullet does not take facts from a different role. A manual edit is kept on the next tailor when the cited source text is unchanged. A changed source does not receive the old edit. Tailoring does not write back to the library. A rewrite the guard rejects can be tried once; a second failure keeps the library wording.
- **Skills:** chips grouped by category (no category last). Add and edit a name (trimmed, required, at most 50 characters) and an optional category (at most 40 characters; blank stores none). Category autocomplete offers this user's categories plus Languages, Frameworks, Tools, Cloud, Data, and Soft skills; a typed category that matches an existing one ignoring case keeps the existing spelling. A name that matches another skill ignoring case is blocked. Delete without a confirmation dialog, then Undo for 8 seconds. The skill leaves the list. Undo puts it back and does not delete it. When the toast ends, or is dismissed without Undo, the skill is deleted. Leaving the page while Undo is open deletes it. If the delete fails, the error shows and the skill comes back. Move up/down within the category.
- Header buttons: Import resume · [1.1] TargetProfiles.
- **Empty:** "Your library is empty" → Import a resume (primary) / Add manually.
- Optimistic updates with rollback + toast on failure.

### `/library/import`
Client state machine `upload → reading → confirm → review` when the library has no entries and no skills. When it already has entries or skills: `upload → reading → review`.
1. **Upload:** one workspace. "Upload a file" or "Paste text". Imports left this month sit beside those controls. The drop zone takes a PDF or DOCX up to 5 MB. A chosen file shows its name and can be removed before reading. **Read resume** stays quiet until a file is chosen, or the pasted text is long enough.
2. **Reading:** an indeterminate panel. The heading stays **Import your resume**. The panel says **Reading your resume**, that roles, projects, education, and skills are being organized, and that this usually takes 10–30 seconds. It does not show a percentage or step through milestones. The indicator is pine green and stays still when reduced motion is on.
3. **Confirm** (empty library only): the heading becomes **Your resume has been read.**, with **We extracted the following for your experience library.** Counts are the sections that have included entries, in library order, then skills. A section with nothing is left out. Warnings stay visible. One line says they can correct the library later, and that tailored resumes use the library. A bullet with no text, or over 400 characters, is shown here to shorten or leave out. **Save to library** waits until each of those is shortened or left out. **Review and edit first** opens the review. **Save to library** → `commitImport` → `/library` (or `/dashboard` during onboarding) with a summary toast.
4. **Review:** the heading becomes **Review your resume**, with **Check the details we extracted before saving them to your experience library.** Extracted profile, entries, bullets, and skills stay editable, each with a checkbox (default on). Warnings from extraction stay at the top. Skills are one compact list: include, name, and category. Wider screens align those in columns; a narrow screen stacks each skill. The category menu lists categories already on this resume, then the usual category names, and includes a field for a category of your own. A typed category is kept. Choosing one does not rewrite the other skills. Long bullets flagged. If the library isn't empty: **Append** (default) or **Replace everything** (confirm). A library that already has entries or skills opens here after reading, and skips the confirmation.
   "Save to library" → `commitImport` → `/library` (or `/dashboard` during onboarding) with a summary toast.
- Errors: bad file → inline; AI failure → retry or paste text (no charge); quota → upgrade modal; nothing found → message.

### `/library/targets` [1.1]
List + editor: name, target role, default emphasis, preferred entries (checklist), preferred skills. Free: 1 target profile; Pro: unlimited. Picking a target profile on the tailor dialog pre-fills emphasis and guides selection.

### `/jobs` — Jobs board
Toolbar: view toggle **Board | List**, a search field (company or title), and **Filters**. Search updates the board as you type. There is no separate Search button. Filters opens source, work mode, priority ≥, has a deadline, and Show archived, at every width. The button names how many of those are on. Each active filter, including a stage, is a chip. Removing the chip clears that one choice. Search, the view toggle, and Filters stay visible at every width. **New job** stays in the header. The toolbar is shared with the list. Choices live in the URL (`q`, `source`, `workMode`, `priority`, `deadline=1`, `archived=1`, `sort`, `dir`, `view`, `stage`). `stage` is `SAVED`, `APPLIED`, `SCREENING`, `INTERVIEWING`, `OFFER`, or `CLOSED`. It shows that one board column at every width. Removing the stage chip, or **All stages** on a phone, clears it. On the list, the same parameter keeps only that stage.

**Board view (default)**
- Columns: Saved · Applied · Screening · Interviewing · Offer · Closed (Closed stacks Accepted, Rejected, Withdrawn, No response, each with its own chip). On a wide screen each column is 320px and does not shrink. The row scrolls sideways when the stages do not fit. Each column is a quiet surface: a header with the stage and the count, then a drop area. An empty column is about 480px tall and grows when a column has more cards. "No applications" sits at the top of an empty column. A fade at the side shows that more stages sit off-screen.
- Card: the role, then the company. A trailing parenthesis that only restates place, work mode, or timezone is left off the card. The job page still shows the full title. Work mode and a short place share one line when they are set. A street address is left off that line. A longer address keeps the city and the country. An unknown work mode is left off. Priority is five stars for the stored value, from 1 to 5, or nothing when priority is empty. A tailored ✓ icon shows if a tailored resume was submitted, and Archived shows when the job is archived. The same primary action as the job header is a text link when the job has one. One status line: the deadline when it is Soon or Past, otherwise the match score (best READY tailored resume). A score reads "Match N of 100". No score reads "Not assessed". A deadline on or before three days from today is marked Soon in ordinary text. A deadline before today is marked Past, in red. The posting summary and requirements stay on the job.
- Drag between columns → `moveJob({ jobId, toStatus, beforeId?, afterId? })` → runs `applyStatusChange` side effects and fractional `boardOrder`. Moving to Closed asks which closed status (+ optional reason). Keyboard alternative: each card's "Move to…" menu, which places the card at the top of that stage.
- Mobile: one column at a time with a stage picker, and that column does not scroll the page sideways. The picker includes **All stages**, which lays the columns in one row that scrolls left and right only. "Move to…" menu instead of drag. The control is an icon; its name is "Move {company} to a stage". Tapping the job title shows "Opening…" until the job appears.

**List view** (`?view=list`): sortable table (Company, Title, Status, Priority, Match score, Applied, Last activity, Deadline). The company cell includes the same primary action as the job header when the job has one. Default sort is Last activity, newest first. Click a column to sort; click again to reverse. Status sort follows Saved through Offer, then the closed statuses. Empty match, priority, applied, and deadline cells show an em dash, and those empty values stay at the bottom of a sort. Match score is the highest score on a READY tailored resume. Inline status uses the pipeline; a closed status asks for an optional reason. Checkboxes archive or unarchive the selection without changing status. A deadline on or before three days from today, in the user’s timezone, is marked Soon in ordinary text; an earlier date is marked Past, in red. Show archived includes archived jobs and marks them Archived.

**Empty:** "No jobs yet. Paste a job posting to accept your first job."

**Filters hide every job:** "No jobs match these filters."

### `/jobs/new`
- The page title is **Add a job**, with “Paste a job posting to analyze its requirements and start tailoring your resume.” The posting is the main field (required, 200–30,000 characters). One line under the field shows the character count and the range 200 to 30,000. That line does not sit inside the field. Leaving the field short replaces the range with “Paste at least 200 characters.” in ordinary text, and does not mark the field invalid. Over 30,000 characters, the line states that limit and the field is marked invalid. A posting inside the range looks like a normal field, with no success mark. The field starts about 200px tall on a narrow screen and about 260px from the `md` breakpoint up. It can be resized vertically. Pasting a long posting scrolls the text area itself so the end of the paste is visible. That scroll is instant. It does not move the page. Reduced motion still applies to page motion.
- **Job details** is visible under the posting: “Optional information to organize and track this application.” Posting URL is full width. Its note says the link is saved for reference only and that adding it does not import the posting. When the URL’s host is LinkedIn or Indeed, including the label right after `www.`, Where you found it becomes LinkedIn or Indeed. A source already set to something else stays. Deadline and source sit side by side from the `md` breakpoint up, and stack below that. Priority stays five stars. Arrow keys, Home, and End change the selection. The value is written beside the stars: “Priority 3 of 5”, or “No priority”.
- The header **New job** control is hidden on this page and stays on every other signed-in page.
- The analyses-left count is read from the server on each visit. Every New job control opens a blank form: the posting is empty, and the page is not left on “Analyzing the posting”. That includes a job that just finished analyzing, the back-forward cache, and the app router cache. Opening the form does not run analysis again.
- Submit is **Analyze & add job**. It stays disabled until the posting is within 200–30,000 characters. A failed save shows “Couldn’t save this job. Try again.” on the form and in a toast, and the typed values stay. Submit → `createJob` (saved PENDING → analysis → fields pre-filled from analysis) → `/jobs/[id]`. The posting can be the only field. Markdown in the paste is kept and analyzed as text. Title, company, location, work arrangement, salary, and reference number are filled from the analysis when those fields were left empty. A blank URL is filled from one link in the posting, or from a link next to “apply”. A URL or other detail the person already set stays. An optional detail outside the allowed values is left empty, and the rest of the analysis is kept. The original posting text is stored as pasted. A stated salary with no currency is CAD when the posting places the role in Canada, including when Canada is only in the title. A currency the posting names, or one already saved on the job, is left as written. Analysis typically 3–8 s; on failure the job is saved FAILED with Retry, and that retry uses the same job. While it runs, the page says **Analyzing the posting**, with a moving indicator and the three lines in order. The current line is marked. It stays on the last line if the call runs longer. It does not show a percentage. Reduced motion keeps the indicator still.

### `/jobs/[jobId]` — Job detail
**Header:** title, company (and location when one is set), status select, and one primary action from the job’s state. Saved with analysis still unfinished has none. Saved with no ready resume is **Tailor resume**, which opens Documents. Saved whose newest ready resume has never been exported is **Review resume**, which opens that resume. Saved whose newest ready resume has been exported is **Preview & export**, which opens that resume. Applied and Screening are **Log follow-up / update status**, which opens Timeline. Interviewing is **Prepare for next interview**, which opens Interviews. Offer is **Review decision date**, which opens Offer. Accepted is **Review decision date** when an offer is saved, and has no action otherwise. Rejected, Withdrawn, and No response have no action. The same action is on each board card and in the company cell on the list. The ⋯ menu holds Edit details (title, company, location, and URL), Add offer while the Offer tab is hidden, Archive/Unarchive, and Delete with confirm, then Undo for 8 seconds. The job stays on this page during Undo. Undo cancels the delete. When the toast ends, or is dismissed without Undo, the job is deleted and the jobs list opens. Leaving this page while Undo is open deletes the job. If the delete fails, the error shows and the job stays. An archived job keeps its status and shows an Archived badge. Priority stars and the posting URL are on Overview. Work mode, employment type, salary, reference number, and the deadline stay in the details form there. Choosing a closed status asks for an optional reason. Choosing Accepted then shows a short congratulations dialog. Opening a job that is already Accepted does not show it again. The same dialog appears on the jobs list and the board. With reduced motion it appears without a fade. The dialog can include an optional testimonial: display name (80), role (120, optional), quote (2,000), and an unticked consent checkbox. Sending stores a `Testimonial` with that exact consent wording, not approved and not featured. It is not shown on the site. The form is hidden when this account already has a testimonial. When the account has a Stripe customer, the dialog also offers “Pause your search?”, which opens the billing portal. The plan still changes only in the webhook.

**Tabs:** The tab row scrolls left and right inside itself. It does not move up, down, or off the screen. Overview is the default. Documents keeps its label and shows a badge with the number of ready resume and letter versions. Zero hides the badge. A visit with `?tab=documents` opens Documents, `?tab=interviews` opens Interviews, `?tab=contacts` opens Contacts, `?tab=timeline` opens Timeline, `?tab=reminders` opens Reminders, and `?tab=offer` opens Offer. While a resume is tailoring or a cover letter is writing, Documents opens on its own unless the URL names Overview, Interviews, Contacts, Timeline, Reminders, or Offer.
1. **Overview:** requirements (Must / Nice). Each requirement is one line, with a kind icon; the kind name is read to screen readers. Keywords, collapsible original posting, priority stars, the posting URL when one is set; details form (source, sourceDetail, work mode, employment type, salary, reference #, deadline, notes with save on blur); "Submitted documents" (which tailor and letter were sent; change via select).
2. **Documents:**
   - **Resume tailored resumes:** [Tailor resume] → dialog: length (1 page or 2 pages, 1 page selected), optional “What should stand out?” (200 characters), a note that the resume is grounded in the experience library, [1.1] target profile. Disabled with a reason if the library is empty or analysis isn't READY. Tailoring view: a pine spinner and a moving bar, with the lines in order. The current line is marked. Finished lines show a check. It stays on the last line if the call runs longer, and does not show a percentage. Reduced motion keeps the spinner, the bar, and the current mark still. It usually takes under a minute, and longer when a rejected line is checked again. The page polls if the user leaves and returns. That progress is the loading state and is not a finished resume. A failed tailor shows that it didn’t finish, with Retry, and does not show a match score, a template, ratings, or Open. Retry starts another tailor with the same length and emphasis. ## Cross-cutting behaviour

| Situation | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Signed out on an app route | `proxy.ts` → `/sign-in?next=…` |
| Resource not owned | `notFound()`, a 404 (never 403). Branded `not-found.tsx`; in-app 404s stay in the shell. |
| Unknown URL | Root `not-found.tsx` with the marketing header and footer. |
| Validation error | `{ ok: false, error: { code: "VALIDATION", fields } }` → inline errors |
| Quota exceeded / Pro-only | `QUOTA_EXCEEDED` / `PRO_REQUIRED` → Upgrade modal naming the limit. A quota also names the Free and Pro allowances. Monthly and 90-day Checkout are in the modal. |
| AI failure | `AI_FAILED` → toast with retry; no charge |
| Concurrent generation | `CONFLICT` → "Already tailoring this job…" |
| Slow AI | Staged progress copy, except resume import, which shows an indeterminate panel; pollable PENDING state survives refresh |
| Unexpected | Toast "Something went wrong." for action failures. Uncaught render errors use branded `error.tsx` / `global-error.tsx` (“Try again”). Server logs ids, codes, and `digest` only. |

## Accessibility & quality baseline
- Keyboard accessible everywhere, including the board (Move to… menu); visible focus.
- Status never shown by colour alone (icons + text); strength tiers always have text labels.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected (progress, count-ups, offer celebration).
- WCAG AA contrast in light and dark themes; works at 375 px.
- Every list has an empty state; every async action has a pending state.
