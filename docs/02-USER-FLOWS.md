## App shell

- **Sidebar** (top bar + menu on mobile): Dashboard · Library · Jobs board · Contacts · Insights · Settings. Bottom: Usage pill ("2 / 3 tailored resumes"; plus "N extra" when tailored-resume bonus credits remain) → Settings → Plan (`/settings?tab=plan`). The pill stays at the bottom of the sidebar while the page scrolls. On a phone it is at the bottom of the menu. [1.1] XP bar and level.
- **Header:** global "New job" button; reminders bell with the count of open reminders that are due today or overdue (popover: Done, Snooze 1 day, Dismiss, and Open job); account menu. The trigger is an icon. The email is inside the menu, with Sign out (and Invite friends [1.1]). On a phone the menu also includes Feedback.
- **Global:** toaster; Upgrade modal (opened by any `QUOTA_EXCEEDED` or Pro-only action); Feedback opens a small form → `Feedback`. On a phone it is in the account menu. On a wider screen it is in the sidebar, above the usage pill, so it does not cover the page. A payment-failed banner when Stripe reports the subscription as past due or unpaid, with a link to Plan & billing to update the payment method; [1.1] milestone toast. Dialogs stay within the visible height and scroll, including when the on-screen keyboard is open.

## Flow A — first-time user (activation)

1. Homepage CTA → `/sign-in?next=/library/import` → magic link (Supabase admin generates it, Resend sends it, five per email per hour) or Google → `/auth/callback` (session only) → first app load `requireUser()` creates the User (stores `signupSource`, `utm*`, `signupReferrer` from the first-touch cookie; `timezone` from the browser via `setTimezone`).
2. Lands on `/library/import` with a one-line welcome: "Let's start by building your experience library."
3. After saving the import → `/dashboard` shows the **Getting started** checklist (4 steps, computed from data):
   1. Build your library (≥ 1 entry)
   2. Accept a job (≥ 1 job)
   3. Tailor your resume (≥ 1 READY tailored resume)
   4. Export it (any tailored resume with `exportedAt`)
   Completing all four sets `onboardingCompletedAt`, shows a brief success message, and [1.1] unlocks the "First tailored resume" milestone.

## Flow B — daily use

Dashboard ("what needs me today?") → complete a reminder or log a follow-up → paste a new posting → tailor a resume → tweak → export → drag the card to Applied (auto follow-up reminder) → later log an interview (auto prep and thank-you reminders) → record the offer → Accepted 🎉.

## Screen specs

### `/dashboard` — Dashboard
- **New user:** First job line checklist (above) + a short "How RoleRefine works" card. Shown while `onboardingCompletedAt` is empty. When all four steps are true, that visit sets `onboardingCompletedAt` and shows the success line once.
- **Returning user:**
  1. **Summary** of jobs that are not archived. Active is every job that is not Accepted, Rejected, Withdrawn, or No response. Interviews is status Interviewing. Offers is status Offer. Urgent counts dated rows in the list below that are due today or overdue: a deadline whose calendar date is today or earlier, an offer whose respond-by is today or earlier, an interview that starts within 48 hours from now, or a follow-up reminder whose due time is before the next local midnight. Undated rows are not urgent.
  2. **Needs you:** one list. The earliest dated item is first, so an overdue date comes before an upcoming one and a sooner date comes before a later one. The same instant uses this order: deadline, offer respond-by, interview, follow-up, document. Undated items come after every dated item, and a follow-up comes before a document. Each row says what it is, why it matters, and links to a specific next step.
     - An open deadline reminder. The date is the job’s deadline when it has one, otherwise the day the reminder is due. The link is that job’s next step when one exists, otherwise the Reminders tab.
     - An offer respond-by date, linked to the Offer tab. An open offer-response reminder is the same row, not a second one. With no respond-by date, the reminder’s due time is the date.
     - An interview whose start is now or later, linked to the Interviews tab. A past interview stays off the list. One that starts within 48 hours is urgent; a later one is still listed.
     - A follow-up: an open follow-up, thank-you, custom, or interview reminder, plus the next step for Applied or Screening when that job has no open follow-up, thank-you, or custom reminder. Reminder rows other than an interview reminder link to the Timeline tab. An interview reminder links to the Interviews tab. Applied for at least 30 calendar days, with `appliedAt` that many days before today in the user’s timezone, says there has been no response. That next step has no date of its own.
     - An unfinished document, with no date. On a Saved job this is the newest ready resume when it has not been exported. On any other open job it is the newest ready resume when it has not been submitted. The link opens that resume.
     Closed jobs stay out of the list. No row links to the jobs board, the dashboard, or a job with no tab and no resume.
  3. **Continue working:** the ready resume that has not been submitted, or the open job, with the latest `updatedAt`. The same instant keeps the resume. Archived and closed jobs stay out, including their resumes. The resume link opens that resume. The job link is that job’s next step, and a job with no next step is skipped.
  4. **Supporting context,** beside the list from the `lg` breakpoint and under the list below that: recent activity (the five newest timeline events on jobs that are not archived; the same instant uses the timeline order) and this week. Each activity row links to that job’s Timeline tab.

Archived jobs stay out of the summary, the list, continue working, recent activity, and this week. This week is Monday 00:00 through the next Monday in the user’s timezone: applications are `appliedAt` in that week, tailored % is the share of those with a submitted resume (an em dash when none were sent), and responses are `firstResponseAt` in that week. Snooze, Done, and Dismiss still live on the reminder itself (header bell and the job’s Reminders tab). Snooze sets the due time to exactly 24 hours from now. A dismissed reminder leaves the lists.

### `/library`
- **Profile card** (header data) with an edit dialog: name, headline, email, phone, location, links (≤ 5), default summary. The “Default summary” heading is one step larger than the contact lines, and the same label in the dialog is larger than the other field labels. From the `xl` breakpoint, the profile card and the completeness card sit side by side. The page uses the wide content width, the same cap as the jobs board.
- **Library completeness card:** completeness is five checks, 20% each: profile complete (name, headline, location, and summary — phone and links are optional), ≥ 1 Experience entry, ≥ 5 skills, ≥ 3 bullets on every recent role, ≥ 50% of all bullets Good or better (score ≥ 4). A recent role is every current Experience entry; if none is current, it is the single Experience entry with the latest start (`YYYY` ranks before any month in that year, and a later year beats an earlier month). Ties use the lower `sortOrder`, then id. Zero bullets fails the Good+ check. **Bullet strength** is a separate line on the same card: “N of M bullets Strong or better” (score 5 or 6). The card lists up to five bullets that are not Excellent, lowest score first, with the tier and the first fix, under that strength heading. Each of those rows opens to show any further fixes and an example of the shape for the first failing check. The example is not a line to paste. A missing number stays “[a number you have]”.
- **Sections** in fixed order (Experience, Projects, Education, Certifications, Volunteer, Other). A section with no entries says “None yet.” and offers add. Entry card: title, then organization, location, and dates on one line, then URL, then bullets. Entry dialog: title, organization, location, URL, start and end as an optional month plus a year (`YYYY-MM` or `YYYY`), and a current checkbox (stores the end as present). From the `md` breakpoint up, edit, move up, move down, and delete sit on the title line. Under `md` those four actions are one menu on that line. Delete still asks for confirmation, then shows Undo for 8 seconds. The entry leaves the list. Undo puts it back and does not delete it. When the toast ends, or is dismissed without Undo, the entry is deleted. Leaving the page while Undo is open deletes it. If the delete fails, the error shows and the entry comes back. Move up/down stays within the section.
- **Bullets:** inline edit (Enter or blur saves, Esc cancels, 400-character counter). Text is trimmed and required, at most 400 characters. Add, delete without a confirmation dialog, and move up/down. Delete shows Undo for 8 seconds. The bullet leaves the list. Undo puts it back and does not delete it. When the toast ends, or is dismissed without Undo, the bullet is deleted. Leaving the page while Undo is open deletes it. If the delete fails, the error shows and the bullet comes back. Each bullet shows a **strength meter** (5 marigold segments, one per tier — Weak fills 1, Excellent fills 5 — with the level written beside it: Weak · Fair · Good · Strong · Excellent) and, on hover or keyboard focus, the reasons ("If you have a number for this, add it to reach Good."). From the `md` breakpoint up, the meter, move up, move down, and delete sit on the same line as the text. Under `md`, move up, move down, and delete are one menu on that line, and the meter stays beside the text. Edit is still the text itself. [1.1] a "Coach" button opens the bullet coach.
- **Skills:** chips grouped by category (no category last). Add and edit a name (trimmed, required, at most 50 characters) and an optional category (at most 40 characters; blank stores none). Category autocomplete offers this user's categories plus Languages, Frameworks, Tools, Cloud, Data, and Soft skills; a typed category that matches an existing one ignoring case keeps the existing spelling. A name that matches another skill ignoring case is blocked. Delete without a confirmation dialog, then Undo for 8 seconds. The skill leaves the list. Undo puts it back and does not delete it. When the toast ends, or is dismissed without Undo, the skill is deleted. Leaving the page while Undo is open deletes it. If the delete fails, the error shows and the skill comes back. Move up/down within the category.
- Header buttons: Import resume · [1.1] TargetProfiles.
- **Empty:** "Your library is empty" → Import a resume (primary) / Add manually.
- Optimistic updates with rollback + toast on failure.

### `/library/import`
Client state machine `upload → reading → review`:
1. **Upload:** drop zone (PDF/DOCX ≤ 5 MB) or "Paste text" tab. Shows imports left this month.
2. **Reading:** staged progress copy, 10–30 s.
3. **Review:** extracted profile, entries, bullets, and skills, all editable, each with a checkbox (default on). Warnings from extraction shown at the top. Long bullets flagged. If the library isn't empty: **Append** (default) or **Replace everything** (confirm).
   "Save to library" → `commitImport` → `/library` (or `/dashboard` during onboarding) with a summary toast.
- Errors: bad file → inline; AI failure → retry or paste text (no charge); quota → upgrade modal; nothing found → message.

### `/library/targets` [1.1]
List + editor: name, target role, default emphasis, preferred entries (checklist), preferred skills. Free: 1 target profile; Pro: unlimited. Picking a target profile on the tailor dialog pre-fills emphasis and guides selection.

### `/jobs` — Jobs board
Toolbar: view toggle **Board | List**, search (company/title), filters (source, work mode, priority ≥, has deadline), "Show archived" toggle, **New job**. Under `md`, those filters sit behind a **Filters** button so the jobs are on the first screen. The button names how many of them are on. At `md` and wider, the filters stay open. Search, the view toggle, and New job stay visible at every width. The toolbar is shared with the board. Choices live in the URL (`q`, `source`, `workMode`, `priority`, `deadline=1`, `archived=1`, `sort`, `dir`, `view`, `stage`). `stage` is `SAVED`, `APPLIED`, `SCREENING`, `INTERVIEWING`, `OFFER`, or `CLOSED`. It shows that one board column at every width. **All stages** removes it. On the list, the same parameter keeps only that stage.

**Board view (default)**
- Columns: Saved · Applied · Screening · Interviewing · Offer · Closed (Closed stacks Accepted, Rejected, Withdrawn, No response, each with its own chip).
- Card: company, title, priority stars, tailored ✓ icon if a tailored resume was submitted, Archived when the job is archived, and the same primary action as the job header when the job has one. One secondary line: the deadline when it is Soon or Past, otherwise the match score (best READY tailored resume). A deadline on or before three days from today is marked Soon in ordinary text. A deadline before today is marked Past, in red. The posting summary and requirements stay on the job.
- Drag between columns → `moveJob({ jobId, toStatus, beforeId?, afterId? })` → runs `applyStatusChange` side effects and fractional `boardOrder`. Moving to Closed asks which closed status (+ optional reason). Keyboard alternative: each card's "Move to…" menu, which places the card at the top of that stage.
- Mobile: one column at a time with a stage picker; "Move to…" menu instead of drag.

**List view** (`?view=list`): sortable table (Company, Title, Status, Priority, Match score, Applied, Last activity, Deadline). The company cell includes the same primary action as the job header when the job has one. Default sort is Last activity, newest first. Click a column to sort; click again to reverse. Status sort follows Saved through Offer, then the closed statuses. Empty match, priority, applied, and deadline cells show an em dash, and those empty values stay at the bottom of a sort. Match score is the highest score on a READY tailored resume. Inline status uses the pipeline; a closed status asks for an optional reason. Checkboxes archive or unarchive the selection without changing status. A deadline on or before three days from today, in the user’s timezone, is marked Soon in ordinary text; an earlier date is marked Past, in red. Show archived includes archived jobs and marks them Archived.

**Empty:** "No jobs yet. Paste a job posting to accept your first job."

**Filters hide every job:** "No jobs match these filters."

### `/jobs/new`
- Posting text (required, 200–30,000 chars), URL (optional), deadline (optional), source (select, optional), priority (stars, optional). Pasting a long posting scrolls to the end of the paste.
- The analyses-left count is read from the server on each visit. New job always opens a blank form, including from a job that just finished analyzing.
- Submit → `createJob` (saved PENDING → analysis → fields pre-filled from analysis) → `/jobs/[id]`. Analysis typically 3–8 s; on failure the job is saved FAILED with Retry.

### `/jobs/[jobId]` — Job detail
**Header:** title, company (and location when one is set), status select, and one primary action from the job’s state. Saved with analysis still unfinished has none. Saved with no ready resume is **Tailor resume**, which opens Documents. Saved whose newest ready resume has never been exported is **Review resume**, which opens that resume. Saved whose newest ready resume has been exported is **Preview & export**, which opens that resume. Applied and Screening are **Log follow-up / update status**, which opens Timeline. Interviewing is **Prepare for next interview**, which opens Interviews. Offer is **Review decision date**, which opens Offer. Accepted is **Review decision date** when an offer is saved, and has no action otherwise. Rejected, Withdrawn, and No response have no action. The same action is on each board card and in the company cell on the list. The ⋯ menu holds Edit details (title, company, location, and URL), Add offer while the Offer tab is hidden, Archive/Unarchive, and Delete with confirm, then Undo for 8 seconds. The job stays on this page during Undo. Undo cancels the delete. When the toast ends, or is dismissed without Undo, the job is deleted and the jobs list opens. Leaving this page while Undo is open deletes the job. If the delete fails, the error shows and the job stays. An archived job keeps its status and shows an Archived badge. Priority stars and the posting URL are on Overview. Work mode, employment type, salary, reference number, and the deadline stay in the details form there. Choosing a closed status asks for an optional reason. Choosing Accepted then shows a short congratulations dialog. Opening a job that is already Accepted does not show it again. The same dialog appears on the jobs list and the board. With reduced motion it appears without a fade. The dialog can include an optional testimonial: display name (80), role (120, optional), quote (2,000), and an unticked consent checkbox. Sending stores a `Testimonial` with that exact consent wording, not approved and not featured. It is not shown on the site. The form is hidden when this account already has a testimonial. When the account has a Stripe customer, the dialog also offers “Pause your search?”, which opens the billing portal. The plan still changes only in the webhook.

**Tabs:** Overview is the default. Documents keeps its label and shows a badge with the number of ready resume and letter versions. Zero hides the badge. A visit with `?tab=documents` opens Documents, `?tab=interviews` opens Interviews, `?tab=contacts` opens Contacts, `?tab=timeline` opens Timeline, `?tab=reminders` opens Reminders, and `?tab=offer` opens Offer. While a resume is tailoring or a cover letter is writing, Documents opens on its own unless the URL names Overview, Interviews, Contacts, Timeline, Reminders, or Offer.
1. **Overview:** requirements (Must / Nice). Each requirement is one line, with a kind icon; the kind name is read to screen readers. Keywords, collapsible original posting, priority stars, the posting URL when one is set; details form (source, sourceDetail, work mode, employment type, salary, reference #, deadline, notes with save on blur); "Submitted documents" (which tailor and letter were sent; change via select).
2. **Documents:**
   - **Resume tailored resumes:** [Tailor resume] → dialog: length (1/2 pages), emphasis, [1.1] target profile. Disabled with a reason if the library is empty or analysis isn't READY. Tailoring view: staged progress, 20–60 s; polls if the user leaves and returns. That progress is the loading state and is not a finished resume. A failed tailor shows that it didn’t finish, with Retry, and does not show a match score, a template, ratings, or Open. Retry starts another tailor with the same length and emphasis. A failed tailor is not charged. Version cards: the version name (a saved label, or "{Company} — {date}"), date, match score, template, 👍/👎, "Submitted" badge when `submittedAt` is set, Open. The card itself opens the resume. Open and the ratings stay buttons.
   - **Cover letters:** [Write cover letter] → dialog: tone (professional, warm, or enthusiastic), length (short, about 180 words, or standard, about 300), recipient name (optional, 80 characters; blank is Hiring Manager), "Why this company?" (optional, 600 characters, strongly encouraged, with the hint "The AI won't invent reasons for you"), align with a READY tailor version for this job. Alignment uses the bullets included in that version, rewritten onto the library snapshot taken for this letter. Disabled when the library is empty or analysis isn't READY. No credits left opens the upgrade modal. Writing view: staged progress; the page refreshes every 3 seconds. That progress is the loading state and is not a finished letter. A failed letter shows that it didn’t finish, with Retry, and does not show tone, a template, ratings, or Open. Retry starts another letter with the same tone, length, recipient, reasons, and aligned resume. A second write while one is under 5 minutes old is refused. A failed write is not charged. A resume can tailor at the same time. Version cards: the version name (a saved label, or "{Company} — {date}"), date, tone, length, template, 👍/👎, "Submitted" badge when `submittedAt` is set, Open. The card itself opens the letter. Open and the ratings stay buttons. Align with a resume uses that resume's version name. The editor, PDF, and mark as submitted stay on the letter workspace.
3. **Interviews** (`?tab=interviews`): list by round, then time. An interview with no date follows the dated ones in that round. "Add interview" and Edit open a dialog: round (1–20; the next round is suggested), type, date and time in the user's timezone (the zone is named beside the field; a spring-forward gap is rejected; a fall-back hour uses the earlier instant), duration (1–480 minutes or blank), link or location (500; an http(s) link opens in a new tab), interviewers (300, free text), prep notes (5,000). Once the scheduled time is in the past, the dialog also has outcome and reflection (5,000). Saving the first date writes "Interview scheduled" on the timeline and an "Interview tomorrow" reminder 24 hours before, and moves Saved, Applied, or Screening to Interviewing. Changing the date moves that open reminder. Clearing it dismisses the reminder. The first outcome other than Pending writes "Interview completed", and a thank-you reminder two hours out when that interview is today in the user's timezone. Delete asks for confirmation, then shows Undo for 8 seconds. The interview leaves the list. Undo puts it back and does not delete it. When the toast ends, or is dismissed without Undo, the interview and its reminders are removed. The timeline and the status stay. Leaving the page while Undo is open deletes it. If the delete fails, the error shows and the interview comes back. [1.1] "Generate prep kit" stays later. Open and completed reminders, including the interview and thank-you ones, are on the Reminders tab.
4. **Contacts** (`?tab=contacts`): people linked to this job, each with one role (Recruiter, Hiring manager, Referrer, Interviewer, Peer, or Other). Change the role in place. "Link existing" searches contacts who are not on this job yet. "New contact" creates the person and links them. Unlink removes only this job's link. "Log outreach" writes a follow-up for today (noon in the user's timezone; details required, at most 2,000 characters) and sets last contacted to that time when it is the latest follow-up for that person. The same person cannot be linked twice. Caps are in `04-DATA-MODEL.md` → Contact fields.
5. **Timeline:** reverse-chronological events with icons (newest `occurredAt` first; the same instant uses the later `createdAt`). "Add entry" (type: follow-up sent, call, email, note, other; a calendar date; details, required, at most 2,000 characters). The date is stored as noon in the user's timezone and shown as that calendar date. Automatic events show the date and time. A status change reads as the two statuses, such as Saved to Applied. Automatic events (job added, status changes, applied, response received, resume tailored, and later letter, interview, and offer events) cannot be edited. Manual entries can be edited (type, date, details, and on a follow-up the contact). "Hide automatic events" filters the list (`?tab=timeline&events=manual`) and does not delete rows. With the filter on and no manual entries, the tab says there are no manual entries yet. A follow-up can optionally name a contact already linked to this job. Saving, changing, or clearing that person recomputes `lastContactedAt` from the follow-ups that still name them. Call, email, note, and other do not take a contact.
6. **Reminders** (`?tab=reminders`): open reminders, soonest due first (the same instant uses the earlier `createdAt`, then id), then completed ones, latest `completedAt` first. Dismissed reminders stay stored and are not listed. "Add reminder" asks for a title (required, at most 120 characters) and a calendar date, including a past date. The date is stored as noon in the user's timezone, with kind CUSTOM. Done sets `completedAt`. Snooze 1 day sets `dueAt` to exactly 24 hours from now and leaves the reminder open. Dismiss sets `dismissedAt`. A reminder that is already done or dismissed cannot be changed. The same actions are on the header bell and on Dashboard Today, for open reminders due before the next local midnight. The bell count is that number. A reminder is a row in the account. It shows when you open RoleRefine. It does not schedule a notification on the phone or computer. The daily email is 1.1. An automatic deadline reminder is titled “Deadline in 3 days: {company}” and shows the job’s deadline date. It is marked Overdue only after that date is before today. It still becomes due 3 days earlier, so it can appear in Today while the deadline is ahead. An offer-response reminder is created from the Offer tab. Changing a deadline, an interview date, or an offer respond-by date still moves the matching open automatic reminder, which can replace a snooze.
7. **Offer** (`?tab=offer`): the tab is listed when the status is Offer or Accepted, when an offer is already saved, or when the URL is already `?tab=offer`. "Add offer" in the ⋯ menu opens that URL while the tab is hidden. The form has base salary (a whole number from 0 through 2,147,483,647, or blank), currency (3 letters), pay period (per hour, per year, or blank), bonus (120), equity (120), benefits (5,000), start date, respond-by, decision (Pending, Negotiating, Accepted, or Declined), and notes (5,000). Blank text and blank dates store null. Dates are calendar dates stored as UTC midnight. Save writes the one offer for this job. There is no delete. The first save writes "Offer received" and moves Saved, Applied, Screening, or Interviewing to Offer. A job that is already Offer, Accepted, or closed keeps its status. A later save does not write another offer event. The decision does not change the status. A respond-by date sets a reminder titled "Respond to the offer" two calendar days before. Changing that date moves the open reminder. Clearing it dismisses the reminder. Caps are in `04-DATA-MODEL.md` → Offer fields.

### `/jobs/[jobId]/resumes/[resumeId]` — Resume resume workspace
Desktop: editor (~60%) + match panel (~40%). Mobile: tabs (Resume · Match · Export).
- **Toolbar:** ← Job · version name (editable, 80 characters; blank uses "{Company} — {date}") · save status ("Saving… / Saved 10:42 / Save failed — retry") · Template (Classic / Modern 🔒 / Compact 🔒) · Copy text · Export ▾ (PDF, DOCX 🔒) · Mark as submitted · 👍/👎.
- **Submitted:** a version with `submittedAt` opens read-only. A banner says when it was submitted and offers **Create revision**, which copies this version into the next version number. That copy is editable. It does not call the model and does not use a tailored-resume credit. The version name can still be edited. Template, summary, includes, bullets, and skills cannot.
- **Editor:** header from profile (read-only, "Edit profile" link); summary (editable, include toggle). The tailored summary is checked for tenure claims and numbers: an "N years" claim cannot exceed the whole years computed from snapshot dates (round down), and other numbers must already be in the library or be that computed tenure. A failing sentence is dropped; if none remain, the library default summary is used. Entries with include toggles (title/org/dates read-only, D-14); bullets with edit, include toggle, up/down, ⓘ source popover, "restored original" badge, strength meter. From the `md` breakpoint up, the meter and move up/down sit on the bullet line with the include switch and the source button. Under `md`, move up/down are one menu on that line. Included entries stay visible. Excluded entries in a section collapse into “N items not included” (“1 item not included” when there is one). Expanding that control shows those entries, and the include switch turns them back on. Included still means that switch is on. Bullets on an included entry stay visible, including bullets switched off. Skills on the resume stay as chips you can remove. Library skills that are not on the resume collapse the same way, and Include adds the skill. A jump bar moves focus to Summary, each entry section that has entries, and Skills. The bar scrolls on its own at a narrow width, and the page does not. The template control changes this screen: Classic, Modern (serif headings and a pine rule), or Compact (tighter type, links on one line). The same choice is what the PDF uses.
- **Autosave** debounced 1.5 s → `saveResumeContent`.
- **Match panel:** Match score (the stored library score; editing this resume does not change it). "How this is scored" says the score reflects the library at tailoring time, is not a hiring probability, and that requirement wording varies. Each requirement is recomputed from the resume on screen: **In this resume** (an evidence ref is in an included bullet, entry, or skill, or the requirement’s key term appears in included text) · **In your library, not in this resume** (that evidence is excluded; Include turns it on without a new tailor) · **Needs review** (no verdict, or a partial verdict with nothing in the resume or the library) · **No evidence found**. A saved row that says missing with the note "Not assessed." is read as no verdict. Each line keeps its note and evidence links. A link scrolls to and highlights the cited bullet, or the entry or skill when that is what was cited. **Keyword coverage** updates as you edit (live %, covered and missing chips). - **Preview:** Preview renders the same PDF as the download, with page boundaries and a page count taken from that file. The template is the one already selected for this resume. If that save fails, nothing is exported and the edits stay. After it succeeds, all three use the same included content in the same section order. Leaving the page with unsaved changes warns; a successful save does not. Choosing a template updates the preview immediately, before that choice is saved. Free falls back to Classic. When the tailor length is 1 page and the regular layout spills past one Letter page, the PDF tightens in that same order (compact spacing, then links on one line, then the minimum type size) and does not drop lines. ## Cross-cutting behaviour

| Situation | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| Signed out on an app route | `proxy.ts` → `/sign-in?next=…` |
| Resource not owned | `notFound()`, a 404 (never 403). Branded `not-found.tsx`; in-app 404s stay in the shell. |
| Unknown URL | Root `not-found.tsx` with the marketing header and footer. |
| Validation error | `{ ok: false, error: { code: "VALIDATION", fields } }` → inline errors |
| Quota exceeded / Pro-only | `QUOTA_EXCEEDED` / `PRO_REQUIRED` → Upgrade modal naming the limit. A quota also names the Free and Pro allowances. Monthly and 90-day Checkout are in the modal. |
| AI failure | `AI_FAILED` → toast with retry; no charge |
| Concurrent generation | `CONFLICT` → "Already tailoring this job…" |
| Slow AI | Staged progress copy; pollable PENDING state survives refresh |
| Unexpected | Toast "Something went wrong." for action failures. Uncaught render errors use branded `error.tsx` / `global-error.tsx` (“Try again”). Server logs ids, codes, and `digest` only. |

## Accessibility & quality baseline
- Keyboard accessible everywhere, including the board (Move to… menu); visible focus.
- Status never shown by colour alone (icons + text); strength tiers always have text labels.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected (progress, count-ups, offer celebration).
- WCAG AA contrast in light and dark themes; works at 375 px.
- Every list has an empty state; every async action has a pending state.
